Our Story

Two Decades of
Building Nepal's Future

Everest Academy was founded in 2003 by a group of Nepali educators who believed that students in Kathmandu deserved access to an internationally recognised curriculum — without having to leave the country.

What began as a single building with 120 students has grown into one of Kathmandu's most respected private schools, with over 850 students, 52 qualified teachers, and a clear record of university placements across three continents.

We have never compromised on our founding principle: that academic rigour and Nepali identity are not in conflict. Our graduates are globally competitive and deeply grounded.

  • Cambridge International Assessment Education affiliate since 2005
  • Fully air-conditioned campus with three science laboratories
  • 98% university placement rate over the last five consecutive years
  • Students enrolled from 14 different countries and districts

Our History

Two Decades, One Mission

From a single building to one of Kathmandu's most respected schools — key milestones in our journey.

2003

Founded in Lazimpat

Everest Academy opens with 120 students, 8 teachers, and one building. Cambridge Primary curriculum adopted from day one.

2005

Cambridge International Affiliation

Formally affiliated with Cambridge Assessment International Education. IGCSE programme introduced for Grades 9 and 10.

2009

New Science Block Opened

A dedicated three-laboratory science wing opens, funded entirely through alumni and parent contributions. Student enrolment crosses 400.

2013

A-Levels Programme Launched

Cambridge A-Levels introduced for Grades 11 and 12 across Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams.

2018

Smart Classroom Rollout

All 28 classrooms fitted with interactive smart boards, high-speed internet, and air conditioning. First school in Lazimpat to do so.

2022

First International University Batch

The largest cohort yet receives placements at universities in the UK, Australia, the United States, and India simultaneously.

2025

850 Students, 52 Faculty

Everest Academy today. Applications are open for the 2025–26 academic year with limited seats available across all year groups.

What We Stand For

Our Six Core Values

🎯

Academic Rigour

We set high expectations and hold students to them. Standards are non-negotiable because the world our graduates enter is competitive.

🌏

Global Perspective

Nepal is our home and our pride. But our students must also think and operate as global citizens. We train both simultaneously.

🤝

Integrity First

In academics and in life. We teach students that how you achieve something matters as much as what you achieve.

💡

Curiosity Over Compliance

We do not want students who memorise. We want students who question, investigate, and build arguments with evidence.

🏔

Nepali Identity

Our curriculum is international. Our identity is Nepali. We celebrate our language, our history, and our country without apology.

🛡

Student Wellbeing

Academic pressure exists. We manage it carefully. A school that produces anxious graduates has failed, regardless of their grades.

Campus Facilities

Infrastructure Built to Teach

Every facility at Everest Academy exists to support a specific learning outcome — not to look impressive on a brochure.

🔬

Three Science Laboratories

Separate Physics, Chemistry, and Biology labs. Each equipped for practical IGCSE and A-Level coursework requirements.

💻

Computer Lab — 45 Stations

High-speed fibre internet. Dedicated Cambridge IT curriculum. Students develop coding fundamentals from Grade 6 onward.

📚

10,000-Volume Library

Physical and digital library. Includes Cambridge-licensed digital resources, academic journals, and a dedicated reading room.

🏃

Multi-Use Sports Complex

Basketball court, volleyball court, and a running track. Physical education is a graded subject, not optional recreation.

🎨

Art & Music Studios

A dedicated visual arts studio and a music room with acoustic treatment. IGCSE Art and Music offered at examination level.

🍽

Cafeteria & Health Centre

On-campus cafeteria with nutritionist-approved menus. Full-time school nurse and a dedicated medical room staffed daily.
