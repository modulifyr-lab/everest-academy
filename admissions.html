Your Child's Place Starts Here

A straightforward, transparent admissions process. No surprises.

How It Works

Three Steps to Enrolment

01

Submit Application

Fill out the online enquiry form. Attach your child's academic reports from the last two years. Submit before the relevant deadline.

02

Entrance Assessment

An age-appropriate written assessment in English and Mathematics. Nursery and KG applicants undergo informal play-based observation only. Results shared within 5 business days.

03

Enrolment & Fee Payment

An offer letter is issued. Confirm your child's place by paying the admission fee. Academic materials and the uniform list are provided at this stage.

Frequently Asked

Common Admissions Questions

Do you offer scholarships?

Yes. Merit-based scholarships offering up to a 25% fee waiver are available for top assessment scorers. Financial need-based waivers are assessed on a case-by-case basis.

Can students transfer mid-year?

Yes, subject to seat availability and a placement assessment.

Is Nepali medium instruction available?

English is our primary medium of instruction. Nepali language is taught as a core subject at every grade. Some subjects can be offered in Nepali medium on request.

What documents do I need?

Birth certificate, previous report cards covering the last two years, passport-size photos, and a citizenship copy of the parent or guardian.

Is there a school bus?

Yes. Routes cover Lazimpat, Maharajgunj, Baluwatar, Baneshwor, and surrounding areas.

Do you accept international students?

Yes, from any country. An English proficiency assessment is required as part of the admissions process.
