A Curriculum That Opens Doors

Four structured programmes, one continuous academic journey — from Nursery to A-Levels.

Curriculum Overview

One Framework,
Four Stages

Everest Academy follows the Cambridge Pathway from Early Years through A-Levels — a single, internationally benchmarked framework that carries students from age three to university entrance without a curriculum change. Each stage builds directly on the one before it, so progression is continuous rather than a series of separate schools bolted together.

Cambridge examinations are recognised by universities in over 160 countries, giving our students a currency that travels with them regardless of where they choose to study after Grade 12.
