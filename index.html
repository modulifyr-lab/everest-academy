Why Choose Us

Education That Goes
Beyond the Classroom

01

Small Class Sizes

Our 1:18 teacher-to-student ratio ensures every child receives individual attention. No student is left behind, and no potential goes unnoticed.

Postgraduate Faculty

Over 70% of our teaching staff hold postgraduate qualifications, trained under Cambridge International Teacher Development programmes.

Air-conditioned smart classrooms, three science labs, a 10,000-volume library, dedicated computer lab, and a multi-use sports complex.
